**Silk strands** is a Brush option that turns one drag into a ribbon of fine, parallel strands that twist around your stroke and taper at both ends. It is good for silky smoke, wisps of hair, light trails and decorative flourishes.

![Two twisting ribbons of thin blue strands over a night skyline, with the Silk strands options highlighted](img/silk-strands-brush.jpg "One drag with the Silk smoke preset")

## Paint with Silk strands

1. Choose the **Brush** (<kbd>B</kbd>).
2. In **Tool options**, tick **Silk strands**, or choose **Silk smoke** in **Saved brush preset** for a ready-made look.
3. Set **Size**: it is the full width of the ribbon.
4. Drag a long, curving stroke. A preview of the strands follows the pointer; the strands are painted when you release.

A click without dragging paints nothing.

## Options

| Option | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Strands | 1–48 | Number of strands across the ribbon. |
| Strand thickness | 0.5–100 px | Line width of each strand. With **Pressure affects size**, pen pressure scales it. |
| Twists | 0–20 per stroke | How many full turns the ribbon makes over the whole stroke. The strands cross each other where it twists. |
| Wobble | 0–100% | A gentle wandering of each strand, for a looser, more organic look. |
| Strand opacity jitter | 0–100% | Makes some strands fainter than others. |
| Seed | 0–99,999 | Chooses the random-looking variation. The same seed and stroke always give the same strands; **New seed** picks another. |

**Opacity**, **Flow**, the brush **Mode**, the foreground colour, pressure options, [Paint symmetry](paint-symmetry) and selections all apply to the strands. Hardness, Spacing, Scatter, Count, jitter, texture and the other tip effects do not.

> [!TIP]
> On a dark background, use a light colour with **Screen** mode and an opacity around 60–70%, like the **Silk smoke** preset. Overlapping ribbons then glow where they cross.

## Good to know

- Silk strands is available for the Brush only, not the Pencil or Eraser.
- The settings are saved with brush presets, your draft and project files.
- Each ribbon is one undo step.
- It is a drawing aid that builds smooth curves from your stroke. It is not a smoke or fluid simulation.

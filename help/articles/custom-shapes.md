The **Custom Shape** tool draws ready-made shapes as editable paths.

![A white speech bubble drawn over a sunset photo, with the custom shape library highlighted in Tool options](img/custom-shape-library.jpg "The custom shape library")

## Draw a custom shape

1. Choose **Custom Shape** from the shape slot (**Rectangle**) in the **Draw & Type** group of the toolbar.
2. In **Tool options**, click a shape in the **Custom shape** library: Arrow, Heart, Starburst, Speech Bubble, Checkmark, Cross, Plus, Lightning, Flower, Puzzle Piece, Drop, Cloud, House, Chevron or Ribbon.
3. Set **Fill**, **Stroke** and **Stroke width** (the same options as the other [shape tools](shape-tools)).
4. Drag on the canvas. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to keep the shape's proportions and <kbd>Alt</kbd> to draw from the centre.

The shape becomes a path layer named after the shape. Because it is a path, you can reshape it with the [path editing tools](edit-paths-and-anchors), stroke it with a brush, or use it as a [vector mask](vector-masks).

## Use your own path as a shape

You can turn any path you draw into a reusable shape:

1. Draw a closed path with the [Pen tools](pen-tools) and keep its layer selected.
2. Choose **Edit > Define Custom Path…** and give it a name.
3. Choose the Custom Shape tool. A **Saved path as shape** list appears under the library. Choose your path there, then drag: the path is scaled to fit the box you drag. Choose **Built-in library** to go back to the built-in shapes.

Saved paths are also listed as **Saved path preset** in the options of the Pen and path tools. Choosing one there adds it as a new path layer; **Update** replaces the saved preset with the selected path layer (also **Edit > Update Custom Path Preset**) and **Remove** deletes it.

## Save a shape preset

**Edit > Define Custom Shape…** saves the current Rectangle, Ellipse, Line or Polygon tool with the foreground colour (and, for polygons, the variant and number of sides) under a name you choose. A dialog asks for the preset name; if another tool is active, the same dialog also lets you choose the **Shape kind** (rectangle, ellipse, line or polygon).

Saved shape presets appear as **Saved shape preset** in the Tool options of the Rectangle, Ellipse, Line and Polygon tools. Choosing one switches to its tool and colour; **Remove** deletes it.

Path and shape presets (up to 32 of each) are saved with the document's settings in your draft and in [project files](project-files).

> [!TIP]
> Need a symbol that is not in the library? The glyph browser has thousands of icons and can insert one as a shape. See [Fonts, glyphs and icons](font-library-and-glyphs).

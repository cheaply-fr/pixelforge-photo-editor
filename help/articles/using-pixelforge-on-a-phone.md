PixelForge works in the browser on phones and tablets. There is nothing to install. The same tools and menus are available as on a desktop, arranged for a narrow screen.

![The editor on a phone with the Properties sheet at the bottom](img/mobile-properties-sheet.jpg "The phone layout: menus at the top, tools on the left, Properties at the bottom")

## The phone layout

- **Top bar:** the **Export** button, the version and server status, and the account button. The menus (**File**, **Edit**, **Image** and the rest) sit on the row below.
- **Document bar:** the document name, and **Save draft**, **Duplicate draft**, **Home** and **Discard draft**.
- **Toolbar:** a scrolling column on the left. Tap a tool to use it. Touch and hold a tool that shows a small corner triangle to choose another tool from its family.
- **Canvas:** in the middle. Use the zoom slider and its zoom-in and zoom-out buttons under the canvas, and the **Hand** tool to move around.
- **Properties sheet:** at the bottom. Tap the **Properties** handle to open or close it. It holds the options for the active tool, the Layers panel and **More panels**.

> [!NOTE]
> On some phones a menu shows only one command at a time. Swipe up and down inside the open menu to reach the other commands.

## Open an image on a phone

The phone layout has no **Open image** button in the top bar. Use **File > Open image…**. Your browser's file chooser usually offers your photo library, the camera and your files. See [Open your first image](open-your-first-image).

To add a picture as a new layer instead, open the Properties sheet and tap **Add image layer** in the Layers panel.

## Touch tips

- With the **Move** tool, touch and hold a spot on the canvas to select the layer under your finger without moving it.
- Brushes can respond to pen pressure when their pressure options are on and the device reports pressure, for example with a stylus.
- Open the Properties sheet only when you need it, so the canvas has more room.

## Limits on phones

Phones have less memory than desktops, so PixelForge uses smaller budgets on them:

| Budget | Phone | Desktop |
|---|---|---|
| Pixels stored in layers | 256 megapixels | 1,024 megapixels |
| Undo history in memory | 128 MB | 320 MB |

The maximum canvas size is the same everywhere: 16 megapixels. See [Large documents](large-documents).

## Keep your work safe

Drafts autosave in the phone's browser. Mobile browsers may clear site data when storage runs low, and private browsing tabs forget everything when closed. Download a project file (**File > Download project file**) to keep a copy you control. See [Drafts and saving](drafts-and-saving).

Editing in PixelForge never needs an account. If you want to reopen your work on another device without moving files yourself, you can sign in with a free Cheaply account and save private **cloud projects**.

Nothing is uploaded until you choose to save a project to your cloud library.

![The My projects panel when you are not signed in](img/projects-panel-signed-out.jpg "More panels > Projects when signed out")

## Sign in

1. Click the **Guest editor** button at the top right, or open **More panels > Projects** in the properties sidebar.
2. Choose **Sign in with Cheaply** and follow the sign-in steps on the Cheaply site.
3. Back in the editor, the account button shows your name.

If the account service cannot be reached, the account button says **Sign-in unavailable · local only**. You can keep editing; your work is still saved on this device. Use **Try again** in the account menu later.

## Save to the cloud

1. Open **More panels > Projects**.
2. Click **Save to my projects**. The message under the button confirms when the cloud copy is saved.

Your local draft keeps autosaving as before, but the cloud copy does **not** update by itself. After further edits:

- **Update cloud project** replaces the cloud copy with your current document.
- **Save as new cloud project** saves a separate cloud copy and keeps the old one.

## Open a cloud project

1. Click your account button and choose **My projects**, or follow **View my projects** in the Projects panel. This opens your library on the PixelForge home page.
2. Click **Continue editing** on a project. It opens in the editor as a local draft linked to that cloud project.

**Refresh projects** reloads the list. **Remove** deletes a project from your cloud library after you confirm; drafts on your devices are kept.

## When the cloud copy changed elsewhere

If you edited the same cloud project on another device, **Update cloud project** reports a conflict instead of overwriting it. Choose:

- **Save as new cloud project** to keep both versions, or
- **Reload latest cloud copy** to replace your current edits with the cloud version (after you confirm).

## Limits and what is stored

- Up to **30 projects**, each up to **16 MB**. Large documents may be too big for the cloud; keep a [project file](project-files) instead.
- A cloud project contains the same editable document as a project file. The **File > File Info / Privacy…** title, description and author are not uploaded, and audio annotations stay on the device.
- Cloud save and the project library need an internet connection.

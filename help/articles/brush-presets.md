Brush presets store a combination of brush settings so you can switch between brushes in one click. PixelForge has two kinds: the four basic tips in **Brush preset**, and the **Saved brush preset** library, which holds ready-made space-art brushes and the presets you save yourself.

![The Saved brush preset library set to Soft glow, with a star-field stroke and a soft glow painted on a night sky](img/brush-preset-library.jpg "The brush preset library with the Star field and Soft glow presets")

## Basic tips

The **Brush preset** menu in the Brush and Pencil options has four tips: **Round hard**, **Round soft**, **Pencil** and **Ink angled**. Choosing one sets the size, hardness, spacing, angle, roundness, tip flips and pressure options. It changes future strokes only.

## Built-in library presets

Open **Saved brush preset** and choose a preset under **Space art**:

| Preset | What it paints |
|---|---|
| Star field | Small hard dots, widely spaced and scattered, with size, opacity and colour jitter. |
| Soft glow | A large, very soft, faint dab in **Screen** mode, for glows and light. |
| Dust / nebula | Scattered soft dabs with opacity, size, slight hue and foreground/background jitter. |
| Smoke wisp | A soft, faint brush with size and opacity jitter and smoothing. |
| Silk smoke | Turns on [Silk strands](silk-strands-brush): a ribbon of fine twisting strands in Screen mode. |

The library presets use your current foreground colour. They look best on a dark background.

## Save your own preset

1. Set up the Brush (or Pencil or Smudge) the way you want it.
2. Click **Save brush preset**, or choose **Edit > Define Brush Preset…**.
3. Type a name and confirm. The new preset is selected and listed under **Saved on this device**.

A preset stores the brush size, tip, opacity, flow, smoothing, scatter, count and jitter, colour dynamics, texture, dual tip, bristle, wet edges, build-up, pressure options, blend **Mode** and Silk strands settings. A preset saved while the Smudge tool is active also stores the Smudge options (Strength, Mode, Finger painting and the sampling options). Presets never contain pixels.

## Rename or delete a preset

Choose one of your presets in **Saved brush preset**, then click **Rename brush preset** or **Delete brush preset**. Deleting asks for confirmation. Built-in presets cannot be renamed or deleted, so both buttons stay disabled while one is selected.

## Where presets are kept

- Your presets are saved in this browser on this device, so they are available in every document you open here. They are not synced to other browsers or devices.
- They are also saved in your draft and in [project files](project-files). Opening a project from another device adds its presets to your library.
- The library keeps up to 32 of your presets.
- If the browser's storage is unavailable or full, the built-in presets still work.

> [!NOTE]
> PixelForge has no custom brush tip shapes: you cannot make a tip from an image or import brush files from other applications.

## Brush presets and tool presets

Brush presets cover brush settings for the Brush, Pencil and Smudge. **Tool presets**, at the top of the tool options, save every option of any tool, including the tool itself. See [Tool presets](tool-presets).

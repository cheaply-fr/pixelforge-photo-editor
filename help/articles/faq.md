## Is PixelForge free?

Yes. The editor is free to use, with no account, trial or watermark. Optional cloud projects with a Cheaply account are free too.

## Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. An account is only needed if you want [cloud projects](cloud-projects) to reopen work on other devices.

## Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are opened, edited and exported inside your browser. They are uploaded only if you sign in and choose **Save to my projects**. See [Privacy and your data](privacy-and-your-data).

## Where is my work saved?

As a draft in your browser's storage on this device, automatically after every change. Download a project file or export an image to keep a copy outside the browser. See [Drafts and saving](drafts-and-saving).

## Can I continue on another computer?

Yes: download a project file (**File > Download project file**) and open it there with **File > Open project…**, or sign in and use [cloud projects](cloud-projects). Drafts themselves do not move between browsers.

## Which files can I open?

PNG, JPEG, WebP and other images your browser can read, SVG, and limited PSD, TIFF, PDF and HEIC files. Camera RAW is not supported. See [Supported file formats](supported-file-formats).

## Can I open Photoshop files with their layers?

No. PixelForge reads only the flattened picture stored inside a PSD. Layers, text and masks are not imported. See [Import PSD, TIFF and PDF](import-psd-tiff-and-pdf).

## How big can my images be?

Up to 16 megapixels (for example 4000 × 4000) and 16,000 pixels on a side, with up to 512 layers. See [Large documents](large-documents).

## Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes, in the browser. The layout adapts to small screens, and memory budgets are smaller on phones. See [Use PixelForge on a phone](using-pixelforge-on-a-phone).

## Does it work offline?

After you have opened the editor once online, it can open and edit without a connection. Cloud features need a connection. See [Use PixelForge offline](offline-use).

## Can I install it as an app?

PixelForge is an installable web app. In browsers that offer it, use the browser's **Install** or **Add to Home Screen** option.

## Do the AI-named tools send my image to an AI service?

No. Commands such as **Remove Background…**, **Generative Fill…**, **Generate Image…**, **AI Denoise…** and **Neural Filters…** run locally in your browser with built-in image-processing methods. They do not upload your image or prompt, and they do not use a cloud AI model, so their results are simpler than those of online AI services. See [Generative and local AI tools](generative-and-local-ai-tools).

## Does PixelForge replace Photoshop?

No. PixelForge uses familiar names, menus and shortcuts, and many features are local approximations of professional tools. Results and supported options can differ, and files are exchanged as flattened images or SVG, not layered PSD.

## Which browsers are supported?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers are what PixelForge is tested on. See [Browser support](browser-support).

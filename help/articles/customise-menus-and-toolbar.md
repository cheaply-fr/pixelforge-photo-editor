These settings change only how the editor looks in this browser. They never change your documents and are not stored in drafts or project files.

## Toolbar

Choose **Edit > Toolbar…**.

![The Toolbar dialog with Tool columns, Category spacing and the Visible categories list with up and down arrows](img/toolbar-settings.jpg "Edit > Toolbar…")

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tool columns | **Auto** uses two columns at normal widths and three on wide screens. Choose **Two columns** or **Three columns** to fix it. On narrow phone screens the toolbar always uses at most two columns so buttons stay easy to tap. |
| Category spacing | **Compact** or **Comfortable** space between the tool groups. |
| Visible categories | Untick a category (for example **Mask Refinement**) to hide it from the toolbar. At least one category stays visible. Use **↑** and **↓** to change the order of the categories. |
| Tool order | Reorder the tool buttons inside each category. Tools of the same family stay together in their press-and-hold menu. |

Click **Apply** to save, or **Reset defaults** to go back to the standard toolbar.

### Tool families

Each toolbar button is a family of related tools. A small triangle in the bottom-right corner of a button (the top-right corner on a phone) shows that it has more tools. Press and hold the button, or right-click it, to open the family menu and choose another tool. The button then shows the tool you chose, and remembers it.

## Menus

Some menu entries are listed but not available yet; they are always shown dimmed. To hide them, choose **Edit > Menus…**, untick **Show disabled roadmap commands** and click **Apply menu settings**.

![The Menus dialog with the Show disabled roadmap commands check box](img/menus-dialog.jpg "Edit > Menus…")

Commands that are only temporarily unavailable, such as **Merge Layers** when one layer is selected, are not hidden. Hiding entries does not remove them from [Search commands](command-search), where they stay dimmed.

## Keyboard shortcuts

You can assign your own keys to tools and menu commands. See [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts).

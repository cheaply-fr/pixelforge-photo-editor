The **Measure** group of the toolbar holds tools that add information on top of your image without changing it: **Color Sampler**, **Ruler**, **Note** and **Count**, along with the [Eyedropper](choosing-colors) and [Audio Annotation](audio-notes). Press <kbd>I</kbd> repeatedly to cycle through them.

Rulers, colour samplers, notes and count markers:

- are saved in your draft and in project files, and every add, move or delete can be undone;
- are never included in exported PNG, JPEG or WebP images;
- are limited to 512 per document in total.

## Ruler

![A ruler line across the landscape with Length, Angle, Δx and Δy in the Ruler options](img/ruler-measure.jpg "Ruler readout")

1. Choose **Ruler**.
2. Drag from one point to another. The line is labelled with its length and angle.

The Ruler options show **Length**, **Angle**, **Δx** and **Δy** for the latest ruler. A line rising to the right has a positive angle; Δy is measured downwards in image pixels.

- **Straighten Layer** rotates the active layer around the middle of the ruler line by the smallest turn that makes the line level, then removes the ruler. Use it to level a tilted horizon on one layer. (To level the whole document and crop the corners, use **Straighten** in the [Crop tool](crop-and-straighten).)
- **Clear** removes the ruler lines.

## Count

![Five numbered count markers on the trees and a note on the sky, with the Count options in the sidebar](img/count-and-notes.jpg "Count markers and a note")

1. Choose **Count**.
2. Click each item you want to count. Each click adds a numbered marker.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Count group | The group new markers belong to. Type a new name to start another group, or pick an existing one. Each group is numbered from 1. |
| Group color | The colour of the group's markers. |
| Label size | The size of every marker label, 8–72 px. |
| Group list | Shows each group with its colour and number of markers. |
| Clear count | Removes the markers of the current group. |

## Note

1. Choose **Note**.
2. Click the image. A small editor opens where you click.
3. Type your note (up to 500 characters), optionally choose its colour, and click **Save**. **Cancel** discards it.

Click an existing note to edit, recolour or **Delete** it, and drag a note to move it. In Note options, **Note color** sets the colour of new notes and **Clear notes** removes them all.

## Color Sampler

![The Color Sampler options listing four samplers with their RGB, hex and alpha values](img/color-sampler-list.jpg "Color Sampler readouts")

1. Choose **Color Sampler**.
2. Click points in the image to place samplers.

The options list each sampler with its **R**, **G**, **B** values, hex code and alpha (α), read from the combined visible image. The values update as you edit, so you can watch a colour change while adjusting. Use the delete button next to a sampler to remove it, or **Clear All** to remove every sampler.

> [!TIP]
> To pick a colour for painting, use the [Eyedropper](choosing-colors) instead; the Color Sampler only reports values.

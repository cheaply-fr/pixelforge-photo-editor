Quick filters are five one-click looks: **Original**, **Vivid**, **Mono**, **Warm** and **Cool**. They are stored with the layer and stay editable, and your original pixels are kept. You can apply them from the top of the **Filter** menu, from **Filter > Filter Gallery…** or from the **Quick filters** section of the Filters panel.

![The Filter Gallery dialog with the five quick filter presets](img/filter-gallery-dialog.jpg "Filter > Filter Gallery…")

## The presets

| Preset | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Original** | Removes the quick filter, so the layer shows its own colours. |
| **Vivid** | Raises saturation strongly and adds a little contrast. |
| **Mono** | Black and white with a little extra contrast. |
| **Warm** | A light sepia tone with slightly richer colour. |
| **Cool** | Turns every hue slightly (by 18°) and lowers saturation a little, for a cooler, muted look. |

A layer has one quick filter at a time; choosing another replaces it. Quick filters have no settings.

## Use the Filter Gallery

1. Select a visible, unlocked layer.
2. Choose **Filter > Filter Gallery…**.
3. Type in **Search filters…** to narrow the list, if you like.
4. Click a card to apply it. The dialog closes.
5. Click **Close** or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to leave without changing anything.

The Filter Gallery currently lists only the five quick filter presets. The other filters are in the Filter menu.

## Use the Quick filters panel

Open the **Filters** panel in the properties sidebar. The **Quick filters** section at the top shows the five presets as swatches; the active one is highlighted. Click a swatch to apply it.

## Quick filters and other filters

A quick filter is separate from the live filter in **Filter effects**, so a layer can have both: for example **Mono** plus a **Gaussian Blur…**. Clearing the Filter effects section does not remove the quick filter; choose **Original** for that.

> [!TIP]
> Quick filters are a fast starting point. For finer control over colour and tone, use the adjustments instead; see [Adjustments overview](adjustments-overview).

The editor has four main areas. The menu bar runs across the top, the toolbar sits on the left, the canvas is in the middle and the properties sidebar is on the right.

![The menu bar and toolbar are highlighted in the editor](img/workspace-regions.jpg "Menu bar (top) and toolbar (left)")

## Menu bar

The menus hold every command, grouped as **File**, **Edit**, **Image**, **Layer**, **Type**, **Select**, **Filter** and **View**. Commands that need a choice from you end with an ellipsis (…) and open a dialog first.

## Toolbar

Each toolbar button is a tool family. Click a button to use its current tool. Press and hold it, or right-click it, to choose another tool from the same family. Tools have single-letter keyboard shortcuts. Press the same letter again to move to the next tool in the family, or hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to go back. See [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts).

## Canvas

The canvas shows your document. Scroll to pan. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> on Mac) and scroll, or pinch on a trackpad, to zoom around the pointer. The zoom slider at the bottom of the canvas and the **View** menu also change the zoom. On a touch screen, pinch with two fingers to zoom and drag with two fingers to pan (see [Zoom, pan and rotate the view](zoom-pan-and-rotate-view)).

## Properties sidebar

The sidebar shows the options for the active tool and the Layers panel. Less common panels, such as History, Filters and Layer styles, are under **More panels**.

## On a phone

On a phone, the menus sit in one row under the top bar, the toolbar stays on the left as a scrolling column and the properties sidebar becomes a sheet at the bottom of the screen. See [Using PixelForge on a phone](using-pixelforge-on-a-phone).

The Pencil paints like the Brush but always with a hard edge, without the soft fade of a brush. Use it for pixel art, crisp outlines and sketching.

![A thin black pencil line and a thicker teal pencil line on a white canvas](img/pencil-tool.jpg "Pencil strokes at Size 3 and Size 12")

## Draw with the Pencil

1. Choose the **Pencil** from the Brush flyout: press and hold (or right-click) the Brush button in the toolbar and choose **Pencil**, or press <kbd>B</kbd> until it is selected.
2. Choose a colour in the **Color** well.
3. Set **Size** in **Tool options**. A size of 1 px draws single pixels.
4. Drag on the canvas.

## How the Pencil differs from the Brush

- There is no **Hardness** option: the edge is always hard.
- The **Pressure affects size** and **Pressure affects opacity** options are not shown.
- There is no **Silk strands** option.

Everything else works as for the Brush: **Mode**, **Opacity**, **Flow**, **Spacing**, **Smoothing**, scatter, count, jitter and colour dynamics, texture, the brush preset library and [Paint symmetry](paint-symmetry). See [Brush settings and dynamics](brush-settings-and-dynamics).

> [!TIP]
> For pixel art, zoom in with the Zoom tool or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + scroll and use Size 1. Use the [Eraser](eraser-tools) at a small size to clean up.

Like the Brush, the Pencil paints only inside an active selection and each stroke is one undo step.

PixelForge is a free photo editor and painting app that runs entirely in your web browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create: open [the editor](/editor) and start working.

![The PixelForge editor with a landscape photo open](img/workspace-overview.jpg "The editor on a desktop browser")

## What you can do

- **Edit photos**: crop, straighten, correct colour and tone, retouch blemishes and remove distractions.
- **Work in layers**: stack images, text, shapes and adjustments, with masks, blend modes and layer styles.
- **Paint and draw**: brushes with pressure, presets, symmetry and textures, plus vector shapes and paths.
- **Apply effects**: more than a hundred filters, including blurs, distortions, lighting and stylised looks.
- **Open and export many formats**: PNG, JPEG, WebP, layered SVG, PSD layers, TIFF, PDF pages and PixelForge project files.

## Your images stay on your device

Images are decoded, edited and exported inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in and choose to save a copy to your cloud projects. See [Privacy and your data](privacy-and-your-data) for the details.

> [!TIP]
> Your work autosaves as a draft in this browser. Export a copy or download a project file when you want a backup you control. See [Drafts and saving](drafts-and-saving).
